## Exploratory charter

```text
Explore   media players and R across browser restarts, and editing the configuration by hand
with      Firefox and Chrome on the fixtures, VLC on the own configuration, the Plasma applet
to find   an R or a reload that does nothing without a clear reason in the log
```

**Time:** about 30 minutes, spread over the run, beyond the steps of the cases. The
charter was written afterwards, from what was explored; no separate session. Counted by
the owner's decision for this run; from the next run on, the session is its own 30
minutes with the charter written first (how-to, ADR 0031 note of 2026-10-16).

**Notes:**

- **Browsers and R:** Firefox and Chrome, closed with the video tab in front or in the
  background, R before and after starting the video. After a restart a browser offers no
  media player until a video plays in the page, so R does nothing
  (`no media player for this app`); a restored tab Chrome has not loaded yet behaves the
  same. Once a video plays, the new player is matched and R works. Not an Apptrol bug;
  the [known issues](../../known-issues.md#r-does-nothing-for-a-browser-until-a-tab-has-played)
  already describe it, and now also Chrome's background tabs.
- **Editing by hand:** `max_volume` on the own configuration (VLC: 150 %, removed, 150 %
  again), each picked up on save. A line added at the end of the file landed under
  `[layouts.default]`; the reload was rejected and the old settings kept, but the message
  reads it as a control and suggests an app ID
  ([#134](https://github.com/Dzobash/apptrol/issues/134)).
- **Buttons:** fast repeated M presses, S1, R1 on an app without a player, muting from the
  Plasma applet: all logged clearly, nothing lost.
- **Second user:** its own configuration logged two warnings about its microphone match;
  that configuration's own matter, not this release.

## Summary

- **Passed:** 11 of 11 (H-20, H-08, H-22, H-37, H-40, H-00, H-01, H-03, H-15, H-29, H-47)
- **Failed:** none
- **Blocked:** none
- **Release:** go. Every case selected by the changed requirements passed. By fix, the
  cases that cover its IDs: #110 (HW-08, SVC-11): H-01, H-47, which recreate its
  situation, a second user logging in; #122 (MUTE-07, INPUT-03): H-22, H-37, H-40, with
  H-37 the hold-to-talk microphone of #118; #124 (MEDIA-01): H-29, the new player found
  after a browser restart, not one silent player next to others; #125 (CFG-06): H-15,
  H-20, ordinary reloads, not a reload after fixing the file's permissions. The last two
  situations are covered by the fixes' automated tests (QA-11), not by a manual case:
  `TestIntegration_MEDIA01_ASilentPlayerDoesNotHideOthers` and
  `TestCFG06_WatchRecoversAfterPermissionsAreFixed`.
- **Found, not blocking:** a clearer message for an app setting placed under
  `[layouts.default]` ([#134](https://github.com/Dzobash/apptrol/issues/134)); and a
  sentence on Chrome's background tabs added to the known issue about browsers without a
  player after a restart.
